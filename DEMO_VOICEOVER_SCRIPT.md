Target: exactly 2:00. Practice once silently (just clicks) to check timing before recording with voice.

Setup before hitting record: servers already running, browser already on `localhost:5173`, Senior Backend Engineer role already visible in the sidebar (don't record yourself finding it).

| Time | Click | Say (read naturally, don't rush) |
|------|-------|-----------------------------------|
| 0:00 | Click **Senior Backend Engineer** in sidebar | "This is the same job I just gave you numbers for." |

## Notes for recording day

- Do one full silent run-through first (clicks only) to confirm nothing's broken and the timing above roughly holds on your machine.
- If a step takes longer live than the timestamp suggests (e.g. resume extraction is slow), just talk a beat longer there and trim your pause elsewhere — don't rush the Extraction Preview explanation, it's the newest/most distinctive part.
- Re-record if you go over 2:15 — a tight, confident 2:00 lands better than a rambling 2:30.
